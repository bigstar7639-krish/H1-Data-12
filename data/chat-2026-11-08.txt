## 1️⃣ What is Poetry? / Kavithai Enna?
- **Poetry** is a *rhythmic* and *imagistic* form of language that aims to evoke feelings, paint pictures, and often capture truths in a compressed, musical way.  
- In Tamil, **kavithai** (கவிதை) literally means “the art of weaving words”. It’s not just about rhyme; it’s about **rasam** (flavour), **bhava** (emotion), and **laya** (beat).

## 1️⃣ What is the heart? / ¿Qué es el corazón?  

- **Anatomy**: The heart is a muscular organ about the size of your **fist** (≈ 8‑10 cm).  
- It’s divided into **four chambers** – two atria (upper) and two ventricles (lower).  
- **Walls**: *Endocardium* (inner), *myocardium* (muscle, the real power‑house), and *pericardium* (protective sac).  

**Tamil**: இதோ, இதயத்தின் 4 அறைகள் – இரு ஆட்ரியா, இரு வென்ட்ரிக்கிள். இதயத் தசை (myocardium) தான் ரத்தத்தை பம்ப் பண்ணும் பவர்.
